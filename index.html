Mingyue Cui

Hi, I am Mingyue Cui, an undergraduate student in Computer Science (with a minor in Finance) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU). In summer 2026, I joined the University of Notre Dame through the iSURE program, where I worked with Zheyuan (Frank) Liu and Prof. Meng Jiang in the DM2 lab on safety for vision-language-action models.

At PolyU, I work on AI safety and model interpretability with Prof. Xingyi Yang, and on LLM and multimodal RAG security in the STiL group with Prof. Kai Zhou, Prof. Bin Xiao and Dr. Yuni Lai. Before that, I was a student research assistant at the PolyU School of Accounting and Finance, working with Prof. Jin Yong.

My research interests lie in AI safety, LLM interpretability, secure generative models, and embodied AI. For my CV, please refer to CV.

Selected Publications (* indicates equal contribution) [Google Scholar]
Learning from Runtime Feedback through Failure-Bank Self-Evolution for Vision-Language-Action Models
Mingyue Cui*, Zheyuan Liu*, Yihan Zhu, Zheyuan Zhang, Meng Jiang
arXiv preprint, 2026
arXiv / code

We introduce FailBank, a self-evolving framework that turns runtime safety-shield feedback into persistent improvement of VLA policies through guarded LoRA updates.

SAE Interventions are Unreliable: Post-Intervention Recovery of Suppressed Behavior
Mingyue Cui, Linghui Shen, Xingyi Yang
arXiv preprint, 2026
arXiv

We show that behavior suppressed by SAE interventions can recover after the intervention, across TPP, WMDP-Bio, IOI, and refusal tasks, while the intervention constraints are preserved.
